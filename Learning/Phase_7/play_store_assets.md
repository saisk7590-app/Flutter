# 🎨 Google Play Store Assets Guide

## 📌 What are Play Store Assets?

Before publishing an app on Google Play Store, you must prepare assets that represent your application.

Think of these as your app's marketing materials.

Example:

```text
App Icon
Screenshots
Banner
Description
```

Without these, you cannot create a professional Play Store listing.

---

# 🎯 Why Are Play Store Assets Important?

Users decide whether to install your app based on:

```text
Icon
Screenshots
Description
Rating
```

before they ever use your app.

Good assets increase downloads.
